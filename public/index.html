Operator Briefing

How to Play

Goal — win 2 of 3 rounds. Each round, bank the most Gold by selling contraband before the market collapses.
On your turn — do one thing
Take 1 card

Draw one good from the market into your hand (max 7).

Take drones

Sweep every drone from the market into your fleet — they don't clog your hand.

Sell goods

Cash in cards of one good for Gold. Rare goods must be sold 2+ at a time.

Exchange

Give 2+ drones from your fleet to take the same number of market cards at once.

Know the market
  • Prices fall as goods sell. Each good's tokens stack high-to-low; every sale pays the top tokens. Sell scarce goods first for the premium.
  • Sell in bulk for a bonus. Selling 3, 4, or 5+ of a good grants an extra bonus token on top.
  • Drones earn Fixer Reputation. Whoever holds the largest drone fleet at round's end takes a +5 Gold bonus.
  • Round ends when 3 goods sell out (watch the SUPPLY meter) or the deck runs dry. Most Gold wins the round and a seal — first to 2 seals wins the match.
